# Hi, I’m Carry Lewis

[中文](README.md) · **English**

**Studying medicine and building tools to test my understanding.**

I’m a medical student interested in medical learning, artificial intelligence, and personal knowledge management. When I encounter a mechanism that is hard to understand, or a task that repeatedly takes too much time, I try turning it into software I can interact with, observe, and improve.

## What I’m building

### [ECG Simulator](https://github.com/CarryLewis/ECG-stimulator)

Exploring how cardiac anatomy and electrical conduction can make medical mechanisms easier to observe and understand. The main branch currently provides cardiac anatomy and conduction visualization; features such as 12-lead tracing are evolving on separate branches.

### [Labwise](https://github.com/CarryLewis/lab-report-assistant)

A lab-report review workspace for teachers. It brings source documents, assessment criteria, and AI suggestions together so feedback can be checked against evidence, while teachers retain the final judgment. Currently in local pilot use and ongoing development.

### [Personal Knowledge Bridge](https://github.com/CarryLewis/personal-knowledge-bridge)

Connecting everyday questions, reading material, and personal reflections. It currently supports one-way synchronization from Notion to local Obsidian notes, making it easier to revisit ideas through their links.

## Questions I keep returning to

- How can medical learning move from remembering outcomes to understanding mechanisms?
- How can AI help people understand material and form their own judgments?
- How can scattered reading and experiences become knowledge worth returning to?

Beyond software, running, reading, and faith shape how I think about human limits and what deserves sustained effort.
